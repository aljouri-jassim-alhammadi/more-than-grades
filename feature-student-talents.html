A Note to Parents and Educators:
“Look for the Spark, Not Just the Score”

When a young person struggles with an abstract academic syllabus, do not diminish their sense of capability. Look for where their eyes light up: they may be a visionary coder, an empathetic community leader, or an expressive designer. Nurturing their intrinsic spark is the ultimate guarantee of their lifelong fulfillment.
