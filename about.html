🌱 Platform Identity & Mission

Cultivating a Campus Culture That Values
Character Before Metrics

More Than Grades was conceived as a supportive digital voice for the unspoken challenges of academic life. We believe authentic education nurtures self-confidence, champions peer empathy, and validates diverse talents inside and outside examination halls.

Our Founding Story

Why “More Than Grades”?

Throughout high school and university life, academic conversations routinely revolve around GPA calculations, percentiles, and test scores. Meanwhile, fundamental human experiences—navigating social isolation as a transfer student, enduring subtle bullying, or suffering severe pre-exam insomnia—remain shrouded in silence.

To address this imbalance, we leveraged generative artificial intelligence to architect an empathetic digital media ecosystem. Through synthetic narrative storytelling, emotive scenario illustrations, and practical psychological guides, we ensure no student feels alone on their academic journey.
