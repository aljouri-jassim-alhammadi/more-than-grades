Entering a new school or transferring between university campuses triggers natural anxiety: Where do I sit? Who do I speak with? Will I find people who understand me? All it takes is one welcoming peer to smile and say: “Come sit with us.”

Mariam’s Story: The First Week in Abu Dhabi

When Mariam's family relocated from Fujairah to Abu Dhabi mid-semester, her first morning at the new campus felt overwhelming. The buildings were vast, the hallways bustling, and existing student circles appeared impenetrable. She spent her first lunch break pretending to read a design textbook to avoid feeling conspicuous.

On the second afternoon, Shamsa noticed her hesitation and walked over warmly: “Hi Mariam! We heard you joined the digital design class. Do you want to join our table and try the cafeteria's famous zaatar pastries?” That casual, hospitable gesture dissolved the knot of tension in Mariam's chest and laid the groundwork for an unbreakable community circle.

💡 Takeaway: A newcomer doesn’t need grand speeches—they just need someone to widen their circle so there is room for them to sit!

Adaptation Roadmap

5 Practical Steps to Settle In & Form Friendships

Psychologist-recommended advice for overcoming relocation anxiety

1

Start with a Simple Question

Questions are natural conversation starters: ask where a specific lab is located, which cafeteria dishes are recommended, or when an assignment is due.

2

Adopt Open Body Language

Resist the urge to hide behind phone screens or keep noise-canceling headphones on continuously; open posture and eye contact signal approachability.

3

Join Clubs & Extracurriculars

Campus clubs (robotics, debate, sports, volunteering, digital arts) immediately place you alongside peers who share your exact interests and values.

4

Resist Rushed Assumptions

A peer who appears quiet or distant on day one may simply be as nervous or introverted as you. Give the environment time to unfold naturally.

5

Stay True to Yourself

You never need to adopt a fake persona or pretend to love hobbies you dislike simply to fit in; genuine, enduring friendships are grounded in authenticity.

✨

The Role of Counselors

If social anxiety or isolation persists past two weeks, speak with the campus wellbeing counselor to help connect you with collaborative study groups.
